I am J. Karuppiah, a chemistry researcher with research experience in nonthermal plasma-assisted catalytic oxidation, catalyst preparation and characterization, and syngas production.

My academic and research work has included doctoral research at the National Institute of Technology Tiruchirappalli, research associate work at IIT Hyderabad, and postdoctoral research at Jeju National University, South Korea.
